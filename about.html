Background

I am a backend engineer with a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science from Ambala College of Engineering and AR (KUK), graduating in 2022. Over the past four years, I have built REST APIs and enterprise applications for banking and telecom clients, working with Java, Spring Boot, Spring Batch, and relational databases.

My work has included a forex trading system, a regulatory data-purging application, and a current telecom and media project at Capgemini. Alongside engineering, I create commentary and short films, bringing the same curiosity and attention to detail to both code and storytelling.
